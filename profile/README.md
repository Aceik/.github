# Aceik – Forward Thinking Digital

Digital solutions that take you further.

Aceik is a Melbourne digital agency and Sitecore Platinum Partner. We bring strategy,
UX and engineering together to build and run digital experience platforms for public,
private and not-for-profit organisations across Australia.

- **Platforms:** SitecoreAI / XM Cloud, Sitecore XP, Umbraco, Next.js on Vercel
- **Services:** strategy, UX and design, DXP builds and upgrades, managed services
- **Recent work:** Brisbane Economic Development Agency (BEDA), ATSE, Queensland Museum Network

The public repositories here are modules and samples from our Sitecore community work,
mostly written for earlier Sitecore versions.
